🕵️ Chapter 2: The 4 Mystery Clues (Why Tests Failed)

When the robot inspectors ran the big 41-minute test suite, 4 specific traps caused 414 tests to break:

Trap 1 • 365 Failures

📸 Missing Photo Album

The camera robots took pictures of screens to check for visual differences, but because the baseline photo album was missing in CI, they failed over and over for 30 whole minutes.

Trap 2 • 403 Forbidden

🔒 Secret Password Mismatch

The contact form robot sent security badges labeled CSRF-Token, but the security door guard was looking for x-csrf-token. The guard locked the door!

Trap 3 • Silent Abort

👻 The Invisible Country Box

An invisible input field was marked required. Because humans and robots cannot click invisible boxes, Google Chrome silently blocked the form from sending.

Trap 4 • Traffic Contention

🚦 The 3-Worker Traffic Jam

Running too many robot test workers at once overloaded the local Vite SSR compiler, causing dynamic imports of entry.client.tsx to time out.
