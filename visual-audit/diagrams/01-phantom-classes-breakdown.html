The Case of the 594 Ghost Stickers

Why did our website's giant title shrink to tiny text, and why did our light switches and tabs stop clicking? Here is the exact visual breakdown.

🔴 The Mistake: Blind Search-and-Replace

An automated cleanup script thought every bracket [...] was "bad code". It blindly renamed 594 class names across 50 files into ghost names like custom-misc-347.

Because nobody wrote the definition for custom-misc-347 in the master design dictionary (theme.css), the browser simply dropped it.

🟢 The Solution: Clean Modern Design Tokens

We restore the exact standard syntax or define official tokens:

  • Use real Radix selectors: data-[state=active]:bg-primary
  • Use fluid responsive clamps: text-display-xl
  • Delete all 594 phantom classes forever.
💡 The 5th Grader Takeaway: If you tell a builder to paint a wall with "Paint Color #459", but Color #459 is not in the paint catalog, the builder will leave the wall unpainted. That's why our buttons and headlines look unpainted!
